Essential items

Pack these for every trip. Hardshell gear and a rain poncho are needed even in the dry season.

Optional extras

Your duffle bag must not exceed 15 kg. An extra porter can be hired for a fee, raising the limit to 30 kg.

Daily gear

Items ticked on the Gear tab appear for each day. Drag items between the day pack and the duffle, or tap an item to move it. From day 2, yellow items have moved since the day before. Clothing you wear all day starts in the duffle, so move it to the day pack if you carry it.
